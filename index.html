Introduction

Why do people travel?

People travel for many reasons, but most journeys begin with curiosity and the desire for a new experience.

LeisureTo rest, recharge, and enjoy beautiful places.
CultureTo discover history, traditions, food, and local stories.
AdventureTo challenge ourselves and step outside our comfort zone.
Use this slide to introduce the topic and purposes of travel.
Benefit 1

Travel opens the door to new cultures.

  • We see how people live, celebrate, and express their identity.
  • We learn to respect differences instead of judging them quickly.
  • Every culture becomes a story we can carry home.
Benefit 2

Travel helps us learn languages.

Even a simple greeting can build connection. When we travel, language becomes real, practical, and memorable.

“One world, many voices, one connection.”
Benefit 3

Travel builds personal growth.

1Confidence to try new things
2Adaptability in unfamiliar places
3Creativity through new perspectives
4Empathy for different lives

Every trip teaches us to solve problems, stay flexible, and trust ourselves.

Reflection

Travel gives our mind space to breathe.

  • Nature helps us slow down and think clearly.
  • Quiet moments turn experiences into lessons.
  • We return home with a calmer and more open mind.
Important Idea

Travel does not always mean going far away.

We can begin with local exploration: a museum, a market, a nearby city, or a place we have never noticed before.

LocalExplore your own city with fresh eyes.
RegionalUnderstand nearby communities and landscapes.
GlobalExperience broader cultures and histories.
Personal Experience

My dream destination is Tokyo, Japan.

"Where ancient temples meet neon skylines — Tokyo is where the future and the past walk side by side."
Benefit 4

Travel strengthens relationships.

Trips create stories that people remember together: getting lost, trying new food, watching sunsets, or solving small problems as a team.

The best souvenirs are not things. They are shared memories.
Conclusion

Travel broadens our perspective.

It teaches culture, language, confidence, empathy, and connection. Whether we travel across the world or explore locally, every journey can help us understand life from a wider point of view.

Final message: The world is open. Go find your story.
