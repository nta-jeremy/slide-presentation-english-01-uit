Good morning everyone.

Today, I would like to talk about the topic “Traveling and Its Benefits.”

Before I start, I want to ask a simple question: have you ever visited a place that made you feel excited, relaxed, or curious? Maybe it was a beach, a mountain, a museum, a famous city, or even a small place near your home. When we travel, we often remember not only the place, but also the feelings and the lessons from that journey.

When we hear the word “travel,” many people think about airplanes, hotels, famous cities, beautiful beaches, or taking photos. These things are part of traveling, but travel is more than that. Travel is not only moving from one place to another. It is also a way to explore new things, meet new people, learn about different cultures, and understand ourselves better.

In this presentation, I will talk about four main ideas. First, I will explain why people travel. Second, I will discuss the benefits of traveling, such as learning about cultures, learning languages, personal growth, and building relationships. Third, I will share my dream destination, which is Tokyo, Japan. Finally, I will explain why travel is important, even when we only travel locally.

Why people travel

Mục tiêu: giải thích động lực đi du lịch bằng ví dụ dễ hiểu.

Reason 1Relax and recharge.
Reason 2Learn culture and history.

First, let’s begin with the question: why do people travel?

People travel for many different reasons. Some people travel because they want to relax. Some people travel because they want to learn. Some people travel because they want adventure. But in general, most journeys begin with curiosity. People want to see something new, do something different, and experience life outside their normal routine.

The first reason is leisure. Many people are busy with school, work, family, and daily responsibilities. After a long time of studying or working, they may feel tired. Traveling gives them a chance to rest and recharge their energy. For example, they may go to the beach to enjoy the fresh air, visit the mountains to see nature, or spend a weekend in a quiet city. These trips can help people feel happier and more relaxed.

The second reason is culture. Many people travel because they want to learn about the world. When we visit a new place, we can learn about its history, traditions, food, festivals, clothes, buildings, and daily life. We can see how people live and what they value. This helps us understand that the world is very diverse.

The third reason is adventure. Some people travel because they want to challenge themselves. They may want to try hiking, camping, riding a train in another country, eating food they have never tried before, or talking to people from different cultures. These experiences may feel difficult at first, but they can also be exciting and meaningful.

So, travel is not only about visiting beautiful places. A journey connects places, but it also connects people, ideas, and experiences. Through travel, we can learn more about the world and also learn more about ourselves.

Benefit 1: Culture

One of the most important benefits of traveling is that it opens the door to new cultures.

Culture is the way people live. It includes food, language, music, clothes, festivals, beliefs, history, and daily habits. When we only stay in one place, we may think that our way of living is normal and that other ways are strange. But when we travel, we can see that every culture has its own beauty and meaning.

For example, when we visit a traditional market, we can see local food, local products, and the way people buy and sell things. We may see dishes that are very different from what we usually eat. At first, the food may look strange, but when we try it, we may understand more about the local lifestyle.

When we visit a museum, a temple, an old building, or a historical place, we can learn about the past. We can understand what happened before, how people lived, and why that place is important. This kind of learning is different from reading a textbook. When we stand in a real place and see it with our own eyes, the lesson becomes more interesting and easier to remember.

Travel also teaches us respect. Sometimes, people judge others quickly because they do not understand them. They may think, “Why do they eat this food?” or “Why do they have this tradition?” But when we travel, we learn that different does not mean wrong. It only means people have different histories and different ways of life.

This is very important in today’s world because people from many countries and cultures often study, work, and live together. If we understand and respect cultural differences, we can communicate better and avoid misunderstandings.

So, every culture we meet while traveling becomes a story. We can bring that story home, share it with others, and become more open-minded.

After learning about culture, travel also helps us learn languages.

In school, we usually learn a language through books. We learn vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation, reading, and writing. This is important. But sometimes, language in a book can feel difficult or not very real. When we travel, language becomes useful in daily life.

For example, when we are in a new place, we may need to ask for directions. We may need to order food in a restaurant. We may need to buy tickets, read signs, ask about prices, or say thank you to local people. These are simple situations, but they help us use language in a real way.

Even a simple greeting can create a connection. If we learn how to say “hello,” “thank you,” or “excuse me” in the local language, local people may feel happy because we show respect for their culture. We do not need to speak perfectly. The important thing is that we try.

Travel also helps us remember language better. For example, if we learn a word from a textbook, we may forget it after a test. But if we use that word while ordering food or asking for help in a real situation, we will probably remember it for a long time. The experience makes the language more memorable.

Travel can also make us less afraid of speaking. Many students are shy when speaking English or another language because they are afraid of making mistakes. But when we travel, communication is more important than perfection. We learn that making small mistakes is normal. The goal is to understand and be understood.

Because of this, travel is a natural way to practice languages. It teaches us that language is not only a school subject. Language is a bridge between people. It helps people from different countries share ideas, feelings, and kindness.

That is why I like the idea: one world, many voices, one connection.

Another important benefit of traveling is personal growth.

Personal growth means becoming better, stronger, more confident, and more mature. Travel can help us grow because it puts us in new situations. In our normal life, we often do the same things every day. We know the roads, the food, the people, and the rules. But when we travel, many things are new.

For example, we may need to read a map, find the right bus, choose a restaurant, plan our time, or ask strangers for help. These things may sound small, but they help us become more independent. Each time we solve a problem, we feel more confident.

Travel also teaches adaptability. Adaptability means the ability to change and stay calm when things do not happen as planned. During a trip, problems can happen. The weather may be bad. A train may be late. We may forget something. We may get lost. At first, these problems can be stressful. But they also teach us to think clearly and find another solution.

For example, if we get lost in a new city, we can use a map, ask someone for help, or look for signs. After we solve the problem, we may feel proud of ourselves. We understand that we can handle difficult situations.

Travel can also improve creativity. When we see new buildings, new landscapes, new lifestyles, and new ideas, our mind becomes more open. We may start to think in a different way. We may compare what we see with our own life and ask questions like: “Why do people do this?” or “What can I learn from this place?”

Most importantly, travel helps us develop empathy. Empathy means understanding and caring about other people’s feelings and lives. When we travel, we can see that people may live differently from us, but they also have families, dreams, problems, and hopes. This helps us become kinder and less judgmental.

So, every trip can teach us many life skills. It teaches us confidence, adaptability, creativity, and empathy. These skills are not only useful for travel. They are also useful for studying, working, and living with other people.

Besides learning and personal growth, travel can also help our mind.

In daily life, many people are very busy. Students have homework, exams, and presentations. Adults have work, deadlines, and responsibilities. Because of this, our mind can become tired. Sometimes, we need time to slow down and breathe.

Travel gives us this chance. When we leave our normal environment for a short time, we can see life from a different point of view. We do not have to think only about our daily routine. We can look around, enjoy the moment, and feel more peaceful.

Nature is especially helpful. When we stand near the sea, we can hear the sound of waves. When we walk in a forest, we can feel the fresh air. When we look at mountains or the sky, we may feel that our problems are smaller. These simple moments can make our mind calmer.

Travel also gives us time to reflect. Reflection means thinking deeply about our experiences. For example, after visiting a new place, we may ask ourselves: What did I learn? What surprised me? What did I enjoy? What do I want to change in my life?

This is why travel is not only about taking photos. Photos are good because they help us remember the trip. But the most important thing is the lesson behind the photo. A beautiful place can help us understand something about life.

For example, a quiet lake can teach us peace. A busy city can teach us energy and discipline. A small village can teach us simplicity and kindness. Every place has something to teach us if we pay attention.

That is why I think a peaceful destination can become a classroom for the mind. We learn not only from teachers and books, but also from places, people, and experiences.

However, I think there is one important idea we should remember: travel does not always mean going far away.

Many people think that travel must be expensive. They think travel means going to another country, buying a plane ticket, staying in a hotel, and spending a lot of money. Of course, international travel can be wonderful, but it is not the only type of travel.

We can also travel locally. Local travel means exploring places near us. It can be a museum, a park, a market, a historical place, a nearby city, or even a street that we have never really noticed before.

For example, in our own city, there may be many interesting places. There may be old buildings with history. There may be small restaurants with special food. There may be local markets where we can see the daily life of people. Sometimes, we pass these places many times, but we do not pay attention to them.

Local travel is useful because it helps us see familiar places with fresh eyes. We may think we know our city very well, but when we explore it like a traveler, we can discover new things.

Regional travel is also valuable. We can visit nearby provinces, towns, or natural places. We can learn how people in nearby areas live, what food they eat, and what landscapes they have. This helps us understand our own country better.

Global travel gives us a wider view of the world. It helps us experience different countries, different languages, and different histories. But whether travel is local, regional, or global, the value is similar: it helps us learn.

So, the value of travel does not depend only on distance. It depends on our curiosity and our attitude. If we are open-minded, even a short trip near home can teach us something meaningful.

Now I would like to share my dream destination. My dream destination is Tokyo, Japan.

I want to visit Tokyo because I think it is a very interesting city. Tokyo combines tradition and modern life. On one side, Tokyo has tall buildings, bright lights, fast trains, technology, and busy streets. On the other side, it also has temples, traditional food, quiet gardens, polite behavior, and a deep respect for culture.

This mix makes Tokyo special to me. I want to see how a city can be very modern but still keep its traditions. I think this is an important lesson because development does not mean forgetting the past. A place can move forward and still respect its history.

One place I really want to visit is Shibuya Crossing. I have seen it in pictures and videos. Thousands of people cross the street from many directions at the same time. It looks very crowded and busy, but it also looks organized. For me, Shibuya Crossing shows the energy of Tokyo. It shows how many people can move together in a big city.

I also want to try Japanese food in Tokyo. Food is one of the best ways to understand culture. I would like to try ramen, sushi, street food, and maybe some local desserts. When we eat local food, we are not only eating. We are also learning about people’s habits, taste, and lifestyle.

Another thing I want to experience is public transportation in Tokyo. Japan is famous for its trains and punctuality. I think using the train system there can teach me about discipline, planning, and respect for time.

If I have a chance to visit Tokyo, I think I can learn many lessons. First, I can learn to be more present and observe small details around me. In a new place, every small thing can be interesting: signs, sounds, food, people, and daily habits.

Second, I can learn that kindness can connect strangers. Even if people do not speak the same language perfectly, a smile, a greeting, or a helpful action can create a good feeling.

Third, I can learn that people around the world are different, but we are also similar. We may speak different languages and live in different ways, but we all need respect, safety, friendship, and happiness.

For me, a travel story is not only about where we go. It is also about what we learn and how we change after the journey.

Benefit 4: Relationships

Mục tiêu: kết nối travel với gia đình, bạn bè, classmates và teamwork.

Another benefit of traveling is that it strengthens relationships.

When people travel together, they spend time with each other in a different environment. This can help them become closer. Family members, friends, classmates, or teammates can create shared memories during a trip.

These memories may come from simple moments. For example, people may remember eating a meal together, watching a sunset, taking photos, walking around a new city, or laughing about something funny. These moments may look small, but they can become very special later.

Sometimes, the strongest memories come from small problems. For example, people may get lost, miss a bus, forget an umbrella, or choose the wrong road. At that moment, it may feel stressful. But later, it can become a funny story. When people solve problems together, they learn how to communicate, cooperate, and understand each other.

Travel also gives people more time to talk. In normal life, we are often busy. Students are busy with school. Parents are busy with work. Friends may be busy with their own schedules. Sometimes, people sit together but still use their phones and do not really talk.

During a trip, people can share more real moments. They can talk while walking, eating, waiting for a train, or sitting in a quiet place. These conversations can make relationships stronger.

Travel can also help people understand each other’s personalities. For example, one person may be good at planning. Another person may be good at finding directions. Another person may be good at keeping everyone calm. Through travel, people can see each other’s strengths.

That is why I believe the best souvenirs are not always things we buy. A T-shirt, a keychain, or a postcard can remind us of a place. But shared memories remind us of the people who were with us. These memories can stay in our hearts for a long time.

In conclusion, traveling has many important benefits.

First, travel helps us understand new cultures. We can learn about food, history, traditions, and different ways of life. This makes us more respectful and open-minded.

Second, travel helps us learn languages. It gives us real situations to practice speaking, listening, reading, and communicating. It also teaches us that language is a bridge between people.

Third, travel supports personal growth. It helps us become more confident, independent, flexible, creative, and empathetic. When we face new situations, we learn how to solve problems and trust ourselves.

Fourth, travel can help our mind relax. It gives us space to slow down, breathe, and reflect. A peaceful place can teach us important lessons about life.

Fifth, travel strengthens relationships. When we travel with other people, we create shared memories, solve problems together, and understand each other better.

Most importantly, travel does not always have to be far or expensive. We can start by exploring our own city, our own country, or any place near us. If we have curiosity and an open mind, every journey can teach us something.

For me, travel is a way to broaden our perspective. It helps us see that the world is bigger than our daily routine. It also helps us understand that people may be different, but we can still connect with each other through kindness, respect, and shared experiences.

So my final message is: the world is open. Go find your story.
